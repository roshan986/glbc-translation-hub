--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Oppressive Black Void Gradients:
   * The previous `--theme-canvas-bg` used `linear-gradient(180deg, #090d16 0%, #05080f 50%, #020306 100%)`.

   * With cards at `#101726` on a `#090d16` canvas, the contrast difference was under 3%. Cards blended into the background, losing structure and causing eye strain during translation sessions.
3) Inconsistent Topbar / Subbar / Hover States:
   * Topbar and subbar had hardcoded colors that did not harmonize with the dark midnight slate theme. Hovering on accordion headers flashed stark white `#f1f5f9`.
